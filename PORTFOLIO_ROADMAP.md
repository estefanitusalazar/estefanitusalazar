## Publication rule

A project should move from a profile case-study page to its own public repository when it has at least three of the following:

- clean source or a safe subset of source;
- screenshots/demo evidence;
- architecture or diagrams;
- reproducible setup instructions;
- tests or validation evidence;
- clear ownership of the material.

Academic rubrics, complete assessment solutions, sensitive data and full original implementations that are worth protecting should remain private.
