# LoCaché — Marketplace / Classifieds Product Concept

## Overview

**LoCaché** is a Chilean classifieds concept built around a simple person-to-person experience: find something, open the listing and contact the seller without turning the platform into a corporate storefront.

Slogan: **“Lo buscai. Lo cachai. Lo comprai.”**

A working site prototype exists with search, categories, listings, favorites/messages/account navigation and a separate pet-adoption section.

## Product direction

The design intentionally avoids becoming:

- a marketplace dominated by businesses;
- a traditional online store;
- an ad-heavy portal;
- an interface overloaded with promotional content.

The intended experience is simple, modern, local and approachable.

## Main UX areas

- search-first home experience;
- category navigation;
- recent listings;
- price and approximate-location visibility;
- favorites and messaging concepts;
- listing publication flow;
- separate adoption experience where animals are not presented as products.

## Identity

The product uses a purple/lilac visual identity and Chilean language in moderation, avoiding forced slang while keeping the interface locally recognizable.

## Portfolio scope

This case study highlights product thinking, UX direction, information architecture and web prototyping. The complete implementation is not published here yet.
